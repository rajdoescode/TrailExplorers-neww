Ladakh Tour Packages

Land of High Passes

Trips starting from ₹8,999 6 trips

Moonscapes, turquoise lakes and monasteries on the edge of the sky. Ladakh is the Himalayan road trip everyone dreams of.

  • 🗓️ May – Sep
  • ✈️ Fly to Leh or drive in
  • ⛰️ 3,500 – 5,600 m

Ladakh sits in the rain shadow of the Himalayas: a high desert of prayer flags, whitewashed gompas and roads that climb over some of the highest passes in the world. Nubra's dunes, Pangong's changing blues and the wild Zanskar valley are all within reach.

Best time
May – September, when the passes are open. Zanskar's frozen-river season is January – February.
Getting there
Fly into Leh, or drive in from Manali or Srinagar over two days.
Altitude
Leh is around 3,500 m and passes go past 5,300 m. Plan a full day to acclimatise.
Perfect for
Road trips, bike expeditions, monasteries and big landscapes.
